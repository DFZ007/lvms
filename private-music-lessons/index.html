Private Lessons

Private music lessons in Las Vegas & Henderson

Private lessons for every age.

One-to-one lessons in eighteen instruments with credentialed faculty, at eight studios across the valley or online. $200 a month for a weekly half hour, $400 for a weekly hour. Every new student starts with a complimentary Initial Assessment.

Ages 5 to 12

Children

Patient, structured lessons that build real skills and a love of music, from a first note to school band and orchestra.

  • Lessons from age 5, 30 or 60 minutes a week
  • Reading music and good technique from the start
  • Help with school band and orchestra music
  • A parent is welcome to sit in on the first lessons
Apply now
Ages 13 to 17

Teens

Serious training for teenagers, whether they play for fun or are preparing for auditions, All-State or a university music program.

  • All styles, from classical to rock and jazz
  • Audition and university preparation
  • Music theory alongside the instrument
  • After-school and weekend times at every studio
Apply now
18 and over

Adults

Private lessons for beginners, returners and serious players, scheduled in the quiet weekday hours as well as evenings and weekends.

  • Weekday morning and midday openings
  • Beginners and returning players welcome
  • Goals that fit your life, not a syllabus
  • In studio or online
