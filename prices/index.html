Questions about tuition

Is the price different for online lessons?

No. Online lessons on Zoom or Google Meet are the same price as studio lessons.

Which lesson length is right for me?

Most young children and beginners start with a half hour. Older and advanced students, and anyone preparing for an audition, usually take a full hour. Your instructor will recommend one at the Initial Assessment.

Does the Initial Assessment cost anything?

No. It's complimentary for every new student, with no obligation to enroll.
