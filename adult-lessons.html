How to start

Three steps, no pressure.

  1. Book your Initial AssessmentComplimentary. Choose a studio or online, and a weekday time.
  2. Meet your instructorPlay a little, talk about your goals and what you love to hear.
  3. Leave with a planRepertoire, a practice routine and a standing weekly slot.
Questions
Am I too old to start?

No. Adults often progress quickly at first because they practise with intent.

What happens at the Initial Assessment?

You meet an instructor, play or try the instrument, and talk about what you want. It is not a test. You leave knowing where you are and what the first months could look like.

I don't have much time to practise.

Your instructor will build a plan around 15–20 minutes a day.

Can I take lessons online?

Yes. Live and one-to-one with the same faculty. You need a stable connection and a camera that can see your hands.

Do I need my own instrument?

Not for your Initial Assessment at a studio. Your instructor can advise on buying or renting afterward.
