Chapter three, previewed

Inside a lesson.

A typical 30-minute lesson. Hour-long lessons follow the same shape, with more time for repertoire.

  1. 0–5 minWarm upScales, rudiments or long tones to settle hands and ears.
  2. 5–15 minTechniqueOne focused skill, taught, demonstrated and practiced together.
  3. 15–25 minRepertoireThe piece of the week: phrasing, dynamics and fixing the hard bars.
  4. 25–30 minReading & planA few minutes of reading or theory, then the practice plan for the week.

From the guide

Talk like a musician.

Scale
Notes played in order, up and down. The warm-up behind almost every instrument.
Etude
A short piece written to practice one technique, like a musical exercise.
Tempo
The speed of the music, measured in beats per minute on a metronome.
Dynamics
How loud or soft the music is, from pianissimo (very soft) to fortissimo (very loud).
Repertoire
The pieces a student can perform, built up stage by stage.
Sight-reading
Playing a piece correctly the first time you see it. Practiced in every lesson.
Rudiments
The 40 standard sticking patterns every drummer learns.
Articulation
How each note starts and ends: smooth, detached, accented.
