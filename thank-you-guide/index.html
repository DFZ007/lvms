What happens next

Three steps to your first lesson.

  1. Read the guideTen minutes over coffee. Chapter two tells you roughly where you or your child will start.
  2. Apply for an Initial AssessmentA few minutes of form. It's complimentary, in studio or online, and there's no obligation.
